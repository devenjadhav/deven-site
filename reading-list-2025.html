My 2025 Reading List

Here's what I'm reading right now! A lot of these are books I've been meaning to read for a while, and I'm excited to get started.

  • The Psychology of Money

    by Morgan Housel

    Timeless lessons on wealth, greed, and happiness. This book explores the strange ways people think about money and teaches you how to make better sense of one of life's most important topics.

    Currently Reading

  • Atomic Habits

    by James Clear

    An easy and proven way to build good habits and break bad ones. Tiny changes in behavior can result in remarkable outcomes.

    Next Up

  • The Pragmatic Programmer

    by Andrew Hunt and David Thomas

    A guide to software development that focuses on practical approaches to programming. It's filled with timeless advice for developers at any stage of their career.

    On Deck

  • Sapiens: A Brief History of Humankind

    by Yuval Noah Harari

    A fascinating exploration of how an insignificant ape became the ruler of planet Earth. This book challenges everything we thought we knew about being human.
